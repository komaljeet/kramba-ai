#217 streamed full backprop at 1B: one 4-layer group, measured on the GPU (validates #216)
Date: 2026-11-19. GPU session 17:21-17:26 (about 5 min). RTX 4050 laptop 6 GB, nothing else on the GPU
(run_all.sh checks <400 MiB used before every run; one process and one log per run).
Script: scripts/bench_streamed_group_217.py. Raw: *.jsonl / *.log here. Tables: analysis.txt (analyze.py).

VERDICT
- One group (layers 4-7) takes 3.545 s per step (spread 3.542-3.546 s over 10 timed steps, 2 processes).
  #216 predicted 3.41 s. Measured is 1.04x the prediction; the pass bar was 1.2x. PASS.
- Full 1B step built from these per-layer times: 22.5 s (#216 likely: 21.8 s) = 2.27x the BCD step
  of 9.89 s = ~241 days for 925,000 steps (#216: 234). Speed bar (~40 s) still passes easily.
- Copies are almost free when overlapped: 2.79 GB in + 1.61 GB out per group cost 0.06 s of the
  3.55 s (first load of the pass + the last write-back). All copies on the compute stream: 3.806 s
  (+0.26 s). Pageable host memory: 3.831 s (+0.29 s).
- Peak VRAM 3.96 GB allocated / 4.53 GB reserved = 70% of the card (includes a 0.82 GB stand-in
  for the tied embedding + its grad). #216: 3.8-4.1 allocated, 4.1-4.4 reserved. Fits.
- Host RAM for this group's state: exactly 1.61 GB (masters 0.673 + momentum 0.673 + AdamW 0.001
  + parked group input 0.268), page-locked without any size rounding. x6 groups = 9.7 GB, matching
  #216's 13.6 GB total for the trainer. The RAM verdict of #216 (does not fit this laptop at full
  precision) is unchanged; this run did not test it at full size.
- The falsifier passed bit for bit, and both must-fail partners failed as they should.

4. OVERLAP FALSIFIER (asked before running: if the side-stream copies were secretly serial,
   would anything change? Yes - moving them onto the compute stream must add ~bytes/rate.)
   Bytes per group 4.40 GB / 13.2 GB/s = 0.33 s expected serial copy time.
   Sync mode measured 3.806 s vs 3.545 = +0.26 s; overlap mode itself still exposes 0.06 s,
   so 0.26 + 0.06 = 0.32 s ~ 0.33 s. The number moved by the expected amount: overlap is real.

WHAT HELD (from #216)
- Per-layer compute times carry over to layer-major order (within 0-3%).
- Copies hide behind compute on side streams; pinned H2D ~13 GB/s.
- D2H is as fast as H2D when pinned (was unmeasured).
- VRAM fits at ~70% reserved.
- The group time (3.41 predicted, 3.55 measured) and so the ~22 s full step.

WHAT DID NOT HOLD / NEW
- No duplex: H2D and D2H share one ~13.2 GB/s pipe. Harmless here (copies are 0.34 s of a 3.5 s
  group) but any design that leans on both directions at once gets half the rate.
- Pageable D2H is slower (10.0 GB/s) than pageable H2D (12.8); pageable mode also makes the host
  block, costing +0.29 s per group (~+1.7 s per step). Pin the host state.
- Backward ~3% slower than the BCD trainer's (0.442 vs 0.430 s per DeltaNet layer).
